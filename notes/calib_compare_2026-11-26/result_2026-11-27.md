## IBM's tendencies

- **Age of the map** (job time minus `last_update_date`, one per job): median 0.41 to 0.63 h on all three devices; but
  on marrakesh 17 of 67 jobs ran on a map more than 24 h old (largest 93.01 h), fez 4 of 35, kingston 2 of 34.
- **Day to day** (one snapshot per device and day, 13 to 16 days): for a qubit of our sets, its largest value over its
  smallest across days is, in the median, 3.30 / 3.54 / 7.81 for readout_error (fez / kingston / marrakesh), 2.18 to
  2.36 for T1, 1.97 to 3.55 for T2. Of the qubits in the device's worst eighth on one day, 0.107 to 0.425 are outside
  it the next snapshot day (T1 turns over most on fez and kingston).
- **Headline against our sets:** the median over our set divided by the device median is near 1 (0.84 to 1.17) on
  every column, kind and device, except the fez sentinel sets' readout_error (1.56). The device headline describes our
  sets' level; it does not point inside a set to the qubit we find.

  sets and are read against the hit rate, not alone (all in `tables.md`).
- Our misses marked by one column alone: cz is the largest single column in every kind (mirror_low 1489, sentinel_dead
  481, scan_listed 277), then readout_error (879, 245, 215).
- sentinel_weak found nothing (0 findings in 109 sets); every IBM mark in those sets is an our-miss by the memo's rule.
- kingston sentinel_dead: 14 sets, 0 findings.
