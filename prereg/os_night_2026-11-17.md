## Step 2: the rule (`python gpu/os_night.py rule DW DC RULE.json`)

The list (theta 1.1, L = 1, 2, 3): P0 the circuit as written (`rus_chain(L, 1.1, m=3)`, the first design); P1 hold
flipped while waiting (`gpu/rus2_night.flip_chain`, m = 2, the second design); P2 P1 and read flipped (`gpu/rus3_night.chain`,
the last bit relabelled 1 - bit, the third design); P3 fewer tries (`rus_chain(L, 1.1, m=2)`).

The rule, fixed order, first match wins (every threshold: the difference positive and at least two SE of itself):
1. Dc shows a late-use loss D_late_nb at position A or B -> P1 for every L;
2. if 1 holds, per L: Dr shows that the qubit holding that L's last output reads |1> as 1 less often than |0> as 0 ->
   P2 for that L. Dr: P(read 1 | |1>) from the arms that prepare |1> and read it at once (Dc "now" control bit for
   chain qubits 1 and 3; Dw "ref" at position B for chain qubit 5); P(read 0 | |0>) from the floor arms of the same
   items (the same qubit);
3. if 1 does not hold: Dw shows unfired conditionals cost D_cond at A or B -> P3 for every L;
4. otherwise P0 (no prescription).
The main job must place each L's last output on the qubit Dr used (checked at build; otherwise the build refuses).

Applied to the recorded diagnostics of 2026-11-16 (marrakesh; read after the fact, a dry run of the rule only): pick
P2 at L = 1, 2, 3 (Dc A +0.535, B +0.243; Dr gaps +0.216, +0.005, +0.005, each beyond 2 SE).
