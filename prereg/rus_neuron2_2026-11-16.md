# Declared: the repeat-until-success neuron chain, second design: fewer waits and the likely value held as |0>

Home Claude, 2026-11-16 (owner 20:46 JST "進めて", on the proposal after the control diagnostic: m = 2 tries and the
outputs held flipped during the waits). Declared with this commit, pushed before the device records of the run are
fetched; a change after the push is a new declared file.

## Why

The first design (`results/rus-neuron-2026-11-16`, m = 3) gave CLOSER WITH CONTROL at L = 3 only, with ff far from
the exact value at every L. The two diagnostics of the same night found that unfired conditionals cost nothing
measurable (`results/wait-diag-2026-11-16`) and that a |1> held through the wait of five mid-circuit measurements is
used as a control with value 0.46 on physical qubit 1 and 0.72 on qubit 3, from time, not from reading the neighbours
(`results/ctrl-diag-2026-11-16`). The loss is toward |0>. Two changes follow from the circuit, not from fitting:
fewer waits (m = 2), and the outputs still in use held flipped (X) during every ancilla measurement, so that the likely
value 1 (theta 1.1) waits as |0>.

## Circuits and job (`gpu/rus2_night.py`; psf-os branch feedforward-2026-11-16, commit 32dc3e5)

Theta 1.1, L = 1, 2, 3, m = 2. Items:
- **flip{L}**: `rus_chain(L, 1.1, m=2)` with, around every measurement of neuron j's ancilla, X on the outputs still
  in use (neuron j's own and neuron j-1's, the control of neuron j's retries) before it and again after it; barriers on
  the ancilla and those outputs around each measurement and each conditional block keep every X pair in the compiled
  circuit, also in the off arm. X X = 1: noiselessly the circuit equals `rus_chain(L, 1.1, m=2)` (full bit-string
  distributions equal, Aer, 200000 shots, L = 1-3).
- **m2_{L}** (beside): the same circuit with the same barriers and without the X pairs.

Each item with the ff, off (conditional blocks removed) and floor arms of `psf_os.feedforward.build`; one ring
sentinel. Placement by rule C (coupling graph and failed marks only), the same chains as the first design (physical
qubits 0-1, 0-3, 0-5). 22 circuits, 4000 shots, 27.6 s by the model; no mitigation, dynamical decoupling and twirling
off. Built by `python gpu/rus2_night.py build DEVDIR OUT` (without `--dry-clifford-sentinel`, which is for dry runs).

## Answers

Exact P(last output 1) = (sin^2 q)^L, tan q = tan^2 theta: 0.9371, 0.8782, 0.8230. With m = 2 the noiseless ff arm sits
below it by the share still failing after 2 tries (ff -0.04 / -0.09 / -0.11; with m = 3 it was -0.02 to -0.03): fewer
waits are paid for with more shots that never succeed.

## Verdict (`python gpu/rus2_night.py score OUT` as of the commit that carries this sentence; unchanged after it)

Per L in {1, 2, 3}, for the flip items, from the job's raw counts, every shot of ff and off counted, none discarded:
d = |P_off - exact| - |P_ff - exact|, SE_d = sqrt(P_ff (1 - P_ff) / N + P_off (1 - P_off) / N);
**CLOSER WITH CONTROL** when d >= 2 SE_d; otherwise **NOT CLOSER**. The m2 items get the same numbers, beside.
