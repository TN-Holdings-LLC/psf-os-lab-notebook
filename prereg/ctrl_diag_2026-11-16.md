# Declared: does a control qubit keep its |1> until a late use, and is a loss from time or from reading its neighbours?

Home Claude, 2026-11-16 (owner 20:34 JST "進めて", on the proposal: measure directly whether a later retry is
controlled by a decayed value). Declared with this commit, pushed before the device records of the run are fetched;
a change after the push is a new declared file.

## Why

The wait diagnostic of the same night (`results/wait-diag-2026-11-16`) found that conditionals that do not fire cost
nothing measurable, while three measurements of the neighbouring ancilla took 0.230 from an output holding |1> on
physical qubit 1 (0.036 on qubit 5), without separating time from the reading of the neighbour. In the neuron chain,
neuron j > 0's retries use neuron j-1's output as a control again, later, after more measurements next to it. This
job measures the control's value at the moment of use, through a target's readout (not the control's), at once and
after the same number of measurements far away or next to it.

## Circuits and job (`gpu/ctrl_night.py`; psf-os branch feedforward-2026-11-16, commit 32dc3e5)

One 6-qubit chain placed by rule C, as the neuron chain at L = 3. Position A: control on chain qubit 1 (neuron 0's
output, the control of neuron 1's retries), target chain qubit 2, neighbours read chain qubits 0 and 2, far qubit chain
qubit 5. Position B: control chain qubit 3, target 4, neighbours 2 and 4, far qubit 0. Per position:

- **now**: X on the control; CX(control, target) at once; measure target, then control.
- **far**: X on the control; five measurements of the far qubit, barriers holding control and target idle meanwhile;
  CX; measure target and control.
- **nb**: X on the control; three measurements of the first neighbour and two of the target (as neuron j's and
  neuron j+1's tries); CX; measure target and control.

CX is written H CZ H. Ideal: target and control read 1 in every shot. No conditionals: each item's off arm is the same
circuit (reported as a repeat, beside); floor arms (sx and x removed) read all zeros. One job: 6 items x (ff, off,
floor) and one ring sentinel; 22 circuits, 4000 shots, 27.6 s by the model; no mitigation, dynamical decoupling and
twirling off. Built by `python gpu/ctrl_night.py build DEVDIR OUT`.

## Pre-run check (before submission)

`python gpu/ctrl_night.py check OUT`: noiseless Aer of every arm but the sentinel; target and control read 1 in every
shot of every ff and off arm, floor arms all zeros. FAIL: the job is not run. On the 12:28 CET marrakesh record
(try-build, before this commit): PASS. The scorer was run end to end on a noiseless local run of the same items (with
a Clifford sentinel): every share 1.000, both predictions and readings "failed" / "neither beyond 2 SE", as they must
be with no loss.

## What is reported (`python gpu/ctrl_night.py score OUT` as of the commit that carries this sentence)

P = share of shots with the target read 1 (the control's value at the CX), every shot of the ff arm counted. Per
position, with two SE of each difference: D_late_far = P(now) - P(far) (time); D_late_nb = P(now) - P(nb) (time and
the neighbours read); D_nb_beyond_time = P(far) - P(nb) (the neighbours read, at equal count of measurements). Beside:
the control's own readout at the end, the repeat (off) arms, the floor, the sentinel.

**Reading rule (declared, for the next neuron design, not a verdict):** per position, "reading the neighbours" when
D_nb_beyond_time is positive and at least 2 SE; else "time" when D_late_far is positive and at least 2 SE; else
"neither beyond 2 SE".

## Prediction (written before the data)

1. Position A: D_late_nb is positive and at least 2 SE (basis: the wait diagnostic's -0.230 on qubit 1 after three
   neighbour measurements).

It holds or fails as a whole; if it fails it is reported as failed.
