# Declared: what costs a neighbouring qubit its value during mid-circuit measurements and conditionals on Heron?

Home Claude, 2026-11-16 (owner 20:24 JST "1から進めて", on the proposal: separate the causes of the neuron chain's
loss with one small job before changing its design). Declared with this commit, pushed before the device records of
the run are fetched; a change after the push is a new declared file.

## Why

In the neuron chain of the same night (`results/rus-neuron-2026-11-16`) the control fired as the circuit says, but the
output lost its value: at L = 1, in shots where no conditional fired, ff 0.607 against off 0.674; at L = 2 and 3 the
off arm sat at its noiseless value while ff fell by 0.22-0.24. Two causes fit and that record cannot separate them:
the waits for the conditional decisions (decay of the output while it waits) and the loss of the output's phase,
which the undo rotation needs. This job measures each on its own, with circuits whose ideal outcome is certain.

## Circuits and job (`gpu/wait_night.py`; psf-os branch feedforward-2026-11-16, commit 32dc3e5)

Two positions of the neuron chain, placed by rule C as there: A = ancilla on chain qubit 0, output on chain qubit 1
(neuron 0's pair); B = ancilla on chain qubit 4, output on chain qubit 5 (neuron 2's pair at L = 3). Per position:

- **ref**: X on the output, measured at once.
- **wT**: X on the output; three measurements of the ancilla, the first two each followed by a conditional X on the
  ancilla (the ff arm; ideally it never fires, the ancilla being |0>); the output measured last. Its off arm is the same
  without the conditionals (measurements only).
- **wP**: as wT, but the output is turned by RY(-pi/2) before the waits and again by RY(-pi/2) after them, so it holds
  a superposition through the waits, as after a failed neuron attempt.

Ideal: the output reads 1 in every shot of every ff and off arm; a conditional that fires on a misread ancilla acts on
the ancilla only. Barriers on both qubits keep the output's gates on their side of the waits. The floor arm of each
item (sx and x removed) reads all zeros. One job: 6 items x (ff, off, floor) and one ring sentinel; 22 circuits,
4000 shots, 27.6 s by the model; no mitigation, dynamical decoupling and twirling off. Built by
`python gpu/wait_night.py build DEVDIR OUT`.

Not measured here: the length of a conditional block that fires (the neuron's block holds an attempt with CZ; here
the block is one X), which adds to the waits of the shots that retry.

## What is reported (`python gpu/wait_night.py score OUT` as of the commit that carries this sentence)

P = share of shots with the output read 1, every shot counted. Per position, with two SE of each difference:
D_meas = P(ref) - P(wT off) (three mid-circuit measurements next to the output); D_cond = P(wT off) - P(wT ff) (the two
conditionals added); D_phase_mid = P(wT off) - P(wP off) and D_phase_ff = P(wT ff) - P(wP ff) (a superposition
instead of |1> through the same waits).

**Reading rule (declared, for the next neuron design, not a verdict):** per position, the larger of D_cond and
D_phase_ff names the main cause: "waits" (next: fewer conditionals, m = 2) or "phase" (next: the question of
dynamical decoupling during the waits goes to the owner, since the standing rule keeps it off).
