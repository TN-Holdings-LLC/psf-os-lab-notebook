## Why

The second design (`results/rus-neuron2-2026-11-16`) gave CLOSER WITH CONTROL at L = 1, 2, 3, with the device's own
loss down to 0.06-0.09 at L = 2 and 3 but 0.22 at L = 1, whose output is read on physical qubit 1. On that qubit the
control diagnostic (`results/ctrl-diag-2026-11-16`) saw a |1> read as 1 in 0.78 of the shots by the qubit itself and
0.98 through a neighbour. Here the last output is not flipped back before it is measured, so that its likely value 1
is read as |0>, and the last bit is read as 1 - bit. The relabelling is fixed by this file before any data; it is a
fixed map of one bit, not a correction learned from data.
